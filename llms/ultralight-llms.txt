# Ultralight

> Ultralight is an AI-native electronic health record (EHR) and operating system for functional, integrative, and longevity medicine practices. It brings clinical data, workflows, and intelligence into a single system — synthesizing labs, wearable data, longitudinal symptoms, and treatment history with a built-in, clinician-reviewed clinical intelligence layer — plus a consumer-grade patient app and automated end-to-end workflows on HIPAA-compliant infrastructure. Headquartered in San Francisco; backed by Anthemis (Female Innovators Lab). Ultralight publishes no public developer portal, API documentation, or machine-readable API surface today.
